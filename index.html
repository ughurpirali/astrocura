ASTROCURA

Health co-pilot for deep-space crews

AstroCura is a 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge prototype for "Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions". It is decision support, not a medical device, and NASA does not endorse it. Daily crew data in the demo is simulated. The blood counts on the NASA OSDR tab are real public data (OSD-569). Hazard and risk taxonomy: NASA Human Research Program. Super Intelligence disclosure: the code and written content were produced with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic) under the team’s direction.
